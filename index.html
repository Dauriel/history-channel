📚 GLOSSARY OF GENUINE 3D PRINTING TERMS 📚
(every single one of these is real. that is the problem.)

Dearest educator: below are terms you must say out loud, to students, with a straight face, for the remainder of your course. We did not invent these. The industry did this to you.

💧 Bed Adhesion
How well the warm, damp bed grips the thing you laid on it. Improved by slathering the bed with a thin film of glue stick. Say "the bed needs to be tacky" to a room of adults. Go on.
💧 Oozing
When the nozzle continues to secrete filament it was not asked to secrete. Also called drooling. Also called weeping. The machine literally weeps.
💧 Purge / Prime Blob
The initial blob the printer must expel before it feels ready to begin. Everyone must watch. It is part of the curriculum.
💧 First Layer Squish
The correct amount of squish. Too little squish and it will not latch. Too much squish and you get elephant's foot. Yes. Elephant's foot.
💧 Stringing
Fine wispy strands left dangling between parts, like the last bite of pizza. Cured by retraction, which is when the material is sucked back in.
💧 Filament Moisture Absorption
Filament drinks moisture from the air and becomes soggy. Soggy filament crackles, pops, and bubbles from the nozzle. Treatment: place it in a dry box with desiccant until it is once again parched.
💧 Wet Sanding
Post-processing. Water. Grit. A gentle circular motion. The slurry that forms is normal. Please tell the students the slurry is normal.
💧 Latch
Extruder idler latch. Enclosure door latch. Spool holder latch. You will say latch nine (9) times per lesson. We counted. 🔒
