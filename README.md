# 💦 THE MOIST ZONE 💦

A deliberately, aggressively cringe website built for the world's most beautiful Russian
3D printing teacher, after a very long week of teaching.

Contains: `moisture`, `hydration`, `latch`, `ooze`, `squelch`, `dollop`, a bilingual
ick section, a hydration nag popup, and a Design Corner featuring Papyrus, Impact,
Comic Sans, non-uniform text scaling, justified rivers, bevel & emboss, nested
marquees and a 3px gutter.

Every 3D printing term in the glossary is real. That's the whole joke.

**Live:** https://dauriel.github.io/moist/

Made with 💧 instead of sleep.
